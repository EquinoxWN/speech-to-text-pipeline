External calls: the sample-clip downloads (`samples.py`) and the one-time model download through
`huggingface_hub` (`engine.py`); everything else is local decoding and inference.

## Defect found and fixed while building

- **Speech lost after VAD joined two utterances.** On a 28.3 s demo (3 s silence, FLEURS en_us_0001,
  4 s silence, en_us_0000, 2 s silence, 44.1 kHz stereo) the first version transcribed only the
  first sentence: Whisper ended the 14 s window after "sentence." and the second sentence was never
  decoded. Root cause: VAD removes the pause between utterances, and Whisper treats a segment that
  ends with a single timestamp as the end of speech in that window. Fix: the resume pass in
  `pipeline.py` (RFC 0001, ADR 0002). After the fix the same file gives both sentences with 2 engine
  calls; `test_two_utterances_joined_after_vad_are_both_transcribed` holds it.

## Known limitations

- On the demo, Silero VAD left the first word of the second sentence ("However") out of its speech
  span, so it was never transcribed; on the same clip padded with digital silence it is kept.
- A stop inside one long span (no join after it) is not resumed.
- Chinese output is in Traditional script against Simplified references, which raises CER.
- Only Whisper tiny has been run; the real-model thresholds are set for it.
